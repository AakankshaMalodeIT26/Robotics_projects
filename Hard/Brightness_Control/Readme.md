Hardware components : 

QBRICK OR MOTHERBOARD

Qbrick or a motherboard is the brain processing unit o f the robot which is powered by a rechargeable battery and turned ON using a switch
• The motherboard contains 6 programmable R J ports to connect sensors and actuators and 2 programmable motor ports to connect the DC/Pump motors, also 1 non  programmable motor port for testing motors.

RJ CABLES

A Registered Jack (R J) is a standardized
telecommunication network interface for
connecting voice and data equipment to a service provider or data
processing device. The most widely implemented registered jack in telecommunications is the Rj11.

LDR SENSOR


Light Dependent Resistor(LDR)is a light-controlled variable resistor whose resistance is decrease when it is exposed to light and resistance
increased when it is dark.

• When a light dependent resistor iis kept in the dark its resistance is very high. This resistance is call as dark resistance.


DC MOTORS

Direct Current (DC ) are electro mechanical devices that use the interactions of magnetic fields and conductors to convert the electrical energy into rotary mechanical energy
The working of  a DC motor is based on the principle that when a conductor is placed in a magnetic field,The conductor experiences a mechanical force
• DC Motor Range varies from  0 to 255
• Electrical DC Motors are actuators that produce a continuous angular rotation that can be used to rotate pumps fans compressors , wheels  etc
. DC Motor rotates in a clockwise and anticlockwise direction.
