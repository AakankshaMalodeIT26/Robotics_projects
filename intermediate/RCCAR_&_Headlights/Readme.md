Hardware Components :

QBrick

Qbrick or a motherboard is the brain processing unit o f the robot which is powered by a rechargeable battery and turned ON using a switch The motherboard contains 6 programmable R J ports to connect sensors and actuators and 2 programmable motor ports to connect the DC/Pump motors, also 1 non programmable motor port for testing motors.

QBit

Qbits are the building blocks used to build a structure for the robot. There are 6 types o f building blocks • 5 Hole pillar • Connector T block Lblock • Angle block(45-degree block) . i block • Additionally, wheels are used to move the robot, castor wheel helps to balance the robot, track Belt and p in are used to build a conveyor system and a freewheel shaft for freewheeling.

RJ Cables

A Registered Jack (RJ) is a standardized telecommunication network interface for connecting voice and data equipment to a service provider or data processing device. The most widely implemented registered jack in telecommunications is the Rj11.

DC Motors

Direct Current (DC ) are electro mechanical devices that use the interactions of magnetic fields and conductors to convert the electrical energy into rotary mechanical energy • DC Motor Range varies from 0 to 255 • Electrical DC Motors are actuators that produce a continuous angular rotation that can be used to rotate pumps fans compressors , wheels etc . DC Motor rotates in a clockwise and anticlockwise direction.


LED

LED stands for Light Emitting Diode. It has two terminals,
 Anode (Positive)- Longer terminal 
Cathode (Negative)- Shorter terminal
An LED is an electronic device that emits light when an electric current is passed through it.
LEDs are commonly used for indicator lights (such as power on/off lights) on electronic devices. They also have several other applications, including electronic signs, clock displays and flashlights.
LEDs can emit infrared light that is not visible to the eye that can be used as an infrared sensor as found on the TV
remote or other electronic devices.
