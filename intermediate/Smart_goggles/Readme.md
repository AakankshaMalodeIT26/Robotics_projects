QBRICK OR MOTHERBOARD

Qbrick or a motherboard is the brain processing unit o f the robot which is powered by a rechargeable battery and turned ON using a switch
• The motherboard contains 6 programmable R J ports to connect sensors and actuators and 2 programmable motor ports to connect the DC/Pump motors, also 1 non  programmable motor port for testing motors.

RJ CABLES

A Registered Jack (RJ) is a standardized telecommunication network interface for
connecting voice and data equipment to a service provider or data processing device. The most widely implemented registered jack in telecommunications is the Rj11.

BUZZER

The Buzzer is a audio signaling device that makes a buzzing sound.
The main working principle is based on the theory that, whenever an electric potential is applied across  a piezoelectric material, a pressure variation is generated. Thus creating an  audible sound wave.

ULTRASONIC SENSOR

The Ultrasonic Sensor measures distance by using sound waves. The sensor consists of a transmitter and a receiver.
• The transmitter emits a sound wave and the receiver receives the waves reflected back from the target as an echo.
• The sensor measures the distance to the target by measuring the time taken between the transmission and reception.
